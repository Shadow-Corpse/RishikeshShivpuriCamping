      OUR STORY

      Rishikesh Camping

      Before starting our own camping business, we worked in the industry and saw the things that often go unnoticed: small details, unsatisfied guests, and the gaps between what is promised and what is actually delivered. That gave us an idea, an idea to create a camping experience where those details matter. Rishikesh Camping was built to help people experince the wild beauty with comfort and care.

      From the first briefing to the final sunset, we focus on making your stay enjoyable, comfortable and worth remembering long after you leave the valley.

      FREQUENTLY ASKED

      FAQ

      Is prior experience required?

      No—our guides adjust the plan to your pace. Beginners are welcome.

      Do you offer refunds or rescheduling?

      Yes. If conditions change, we'll reschedule when possible or provide a refund according to your booking.

      What safety measures are in place?

      Don't worry. All activities include certified guides, top-grade equipment, safety briefings, and first-aid kits. Our team is trained in emergency response and river rescue protocols.

      Can we explore the surrounding areas during our stay?

      Sure you can! We encourage our guests to explore the beautiful surroundings and experience the local culture, infact there is a stream flowing down the hill you can go and enjoy there.

      Can I customize my package?

      Absolutely! Contact us with your preferences and we'll tailor the itinerary, activities, and duration to suit your group perfectly.

      Do you offer group discounts?

      Yes—groups receive special pricing. Get in touch and we'll create a custom quote with the best rates for your group.

      What is the best time to visit Rishikesh?

      The ideal season is September to June. Monsoon season (July–August) may affect river activities, so we advise checking availability ahead of time.
